Support

Dear Jane is a Jane Austen companion app - Regency scandals, Austen quizzes, and Society Honours. Here is how to reach us and a few quick answers.

Contact

Prefer the in-app Send a note option in Settings when you can - it reaches us with useful device context and avoids exposing an email address to scrapers.

Email: Loading…

We read every genuine note. Please include your device type (iPhone or Android) and a short description of the issue. We aim to reply within a few days.

Quick answers

  • Where is my progress stored? On your device only. There are no accounts. Uninstalling or using Fresh start in Settings clears local progress.
  • I found a bug or a wrong quiz answer. Use Send a note in the app, or email us with the quiz or scandal title and what looked wrong.
  • How do I delete my data? Use Fresh start in Settings for local progress. For analytics, crash, or feedback data held by our processors, email us - see the Privacy Policy.
  • Is Dear Jane official Jane Austen content? No. It is an unofficial fan work inspired by the novels. No affiliation is claimed.
