Dear Jane Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frances Maxwell (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Dear Jane mobile application and the related website at dearjaneapp.co.uk (together, the “Services”).

In short: Dear Jane has no accounts and no sign-up. Your game progress stays on your device. We use PostHog for product analytics and Sentry for crash reporting, diagnostics, and limited session replay. If you send optional feedback, that note (and any name or email you include) goes to Sentry. Voluntary donations via Buy Me a Coffee are handled by that service and its payment provider, not inside Dear Jane.

Questions? Email info@dearjaneapp.co.uk. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

1. What information do we collect?

Information stored on your device

Dear Jane stores your progress and preferences locally on your device (for example via on-device storage). This can include:

  • Completed scandals and quizzes, and related outcomes
  • Which Austen novels you have marked as read
  • Theme preference (light, dark, or system)
  • Onboarding and in-app notice dismissals
  • A random analytics install ID used only to keep analytics consistent across app launches

This local game data is not uploaded to our own servers. The analytics install ID and product events described below may be sent to our analytics provider when analytics are enabled.

Information you choose to send us

If you use the in-app “Send a note” feedback form, you may optionally provide a name and email address along with your message. That feedback is sent to Sentry so we can read and respond to it. You do not need to provide a name or email to use Dear Jane.

Product analytics (PostHog)

In preview and production builds, Dear Jane uses PostHog (EU cloud) to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Analytics are disabled in local development builds.

PostHog may receive:

  • A durable, randomly generated analytics install ID
  • Device and app technical details (such as device model, OS, app version)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not GPS)
  • App screens viewed and product events (for example starting or completing a scandal or quiz, filter choices, sharing taps, theme changes, and onboarding completion)
  • Limited in-app choice data tied to those events (for example verdicts, guessed characters, quiz results, and, for some knowledge quizzes, which questions were missed)
  • A small set of profile properties we set for analytics (for example novels read count, theme preference, and aficionado level)

We do not ask for your real name, and we do not use advertising identifiers for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Crash reporting, diagnostics, and session replay (Sentry)

Dear Jane uses Sentry to diagnose crashes and errors. Sentry may receive:

  • Error and crash reports, stack traces, and diagnostic logs
  • Device and app technical details
  • IP address and related default diagnostic context
  • Performance traces used to investigate reliability issues
  • Session replay recordings for a sample of sessions (and more often when an error occurs). Replay can capture what appeared on screen in the app around the time of the session or error.
  • Optional name, email, and message if you submit feedback

Donations (Buy Me a Coffee / Stripe)

Dear Jane links out to Buy Me a Coffee for optional support. Payments there are processed by Buy Me a Coffee and its payment provider (Stripe). We do not collect or store your card details inside Dear Jane, and donations are not required to use the app.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No precise GPS location
  • No push notification tokens (Dear Jane does not send push notifications)
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access for data collection
  • No sale of personal information
  • No intentional collection of sensitive special-category data

2. How do we use your information?

We process information to:

  • Provide and operate Dear Jane on your device
  • Understand usage trends and improve features, content, and reliability
  • Diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance problems
  • Read and respond to feedback you send
  • Protect the Services against abuse or technical misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

3. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

If you are in the EEA or UK, we rely on these legal bases under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

  • Legitimate interests - to operate, secure, and improve Dear Jane through analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and limited session replay, in a way that we believe does not override your rights.
  • Consent - where you choose to send feedback with optional contact details, or where consent is otherwise required by law.
  • Legal obligations - where we must process information to comply with applicable law.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent where we rely on consent, by contacting us at info@dearjaneapp.co.uk.

4. When and with whom do we share information?

We share information with service providers who process it on our behalf to run the Services:

  • PostHog - product analytics
  • Sentry - crash reporting, diagnostics, session replay, and feedback
  • Buy Me a Coffee / Stripe - only if you choose to make a donation on their site
  • Apple / Google - if you install beta or store builds through TestFlight, the App Store, or Google Play, those platforms may process install and distribution data under their own terms

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a genuine business transfer (for example a sale of assets). We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. How long do we keep information?

  • On-device game data stays on your device until you use Fresh start in Settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app (noting that some secure-storage values may persist across reinstall on the same device depending on platform behaviour).
  • Analytics and diagnostics are retained in PostHog and Sentry according to our configured retention in those tools, and no longer than needed for the purposes above unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Feedback is retained as long as needed to review and respond to your note and keep a record of support issues.

6. How do we keep information safe?

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including relying on reputable providers and limiting what we send off-device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Do we collect information from minors?

Dear Jane is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Services, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. EEA/UK residents may also complain to their local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

To exercise these rights, email info@dearjaneapp.co.uk.

Fresh start in the app deletes local game progress and preferences on your device. It does not by itself delete analytics or crash/feedback records already held by PostHog or Sentry. To request deletion of server-side data associated with your install or feedback, contact us.
